## SEO Updates

- **Hyphenated domain names confirmed as SEO-neutral by Google**: Google has officially stated that hyphenated domains carry no SEO penalty, directly contradicting a long-held industry assumption. If your spiritual site uses or is considering a hyphenated domain (e.g., `hindu-wisdom.com`), this is no longer a ranking concern.
- **Google has updated its SEO hiring guidance** to now actively encourage FTC complaints against deceptive SEO practitioners, while simultaneously cautioning against AI-powered SEO services and third-party SEO tools that make misleading claims. Vet any SEO vendor or tool you use for your spirituality site more carefully than before.
- **Google is positioning itself as the sole authoritative source** for SEO, AEO, and GEO guidance, explicitly questioning the reliability of third-party SEO tools and data. Recommendations or audits from popular tools (e.g., Ahrefs, Semrush signals) should be cross-referenced against Google Search Central documentation directly.

## AEO Updates (Answer Engine Optimization)

- **Google has published new guidance formally claiming authority over AEO best practices**, signaling that the definition and rules of Answer Engine Optimization are now being shaped directly by Google rather than the SEO industry. This is a structural shift — third-party AEO frameworks may diverge from what Google actually rewards.
- **CMO/CIO misalignment around AI agents is emerging as a real AEO execution risk**: Organizations are failing to operationalize AEO because technical and marketing teams are solving different problems with AI agent strategies. For a smaller spiritual content site, this is a signal to ensure your content structure (FAQs, structured Q&A, schema) is built for answer retrieval, not just traffic volume.

## GEO Updates (Generative Engine Optimization / AI Search)

- **Google is beginning to offer websites an opt-out from AI Search (AI Overviews/generative features)**, but critically, the click data needed to assess whether opting out is beneficial is not yet available to publishers. Do not make an opt-out decision yet — wait for Google to release AI search visibility data before evaluating the trade-off for your spiritual content.
- **Google is testing AI visibility reports and an opt-out toggle** inside Search Console (currently in testing; UK regulators are requiring publisher controls). Monitor Search Console closely for this feature becoming available in your region.
- **Tying AI search to measurable performance is now a priority concern**: Standard click/impression KPIs do not capture AI search visibility. Spiritual content sites that rely on informational queries (e.g., "meaning of Diwali," "what is karma") are likely being surfaced in AI Overviews without generating tracked clicks — meaning your actual reach may be significantly underreported in current analytics.

## Key Takeaway for a Spiritual/Religious Content Website

The single most important action this month is to **hold off on opting out of Google's AI Search features, but begin preparing to measure their impact** — because spiritual and Hindu content is heavily informational and definitional in nature (rituals, festivals, scripture explanations, deity descriptions), making it highly likely to be cited in AI Overviews without generating traditional clicks. Set up a baseline now by documenting your current impressions and traffic for your top informational queries in Google Search Console, so that when Google releases AI visibility reporting data (currently in testing), you have a before/after comparison ready and can make an informed, data-driven decision about whether AI search inclusion is helping or cannibalizing your audience.
