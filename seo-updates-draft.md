## AEO Updates (Answer Engine Optimization)

- **Structured data (schema markup) is now explicitly framed as an entity trust signal for AI citation**, not just a ranking factor. Consistent schema across your content strengthens how AI systems identify and verify your site as a credible, citable source — particularly relevant for author-driven or topical authority content like spiritual teachings, deity descriptions, or scripture explanations.
- **Author schema and entity consistency are specifically highlighted** as mechanisms that help answer engines trust and cite your content. For a spirituality site, this means ensuring every article has properly implemented `Person` schema for authors, `Article` schema, and ideally `Organization` schema linking back to a consistent brand entity.

## GEO Updates (Generative Engine Optimization / AI Search)

- **Google Search Console AI Reports are now live worldwide** — this is the first time site owners have direct data on AI Overview and AI Mode visibility. This creates a measurable feedback loop for GEO efforts that previously did not exist.
- **John Mueller personally tested whether AI crawlers request markdown format from sites** — his findings suggest that serving markdown to AI crawlers is not yet a reliable or confirmed signal, meaning investing heavily in markdown-only formatting strategies is premature. Focus on clean, well-structured HTML content instead.
- **Data-driven, research-backed content is identified as the primary format for earning AI Overview citations** — a documented digital PR case study showed that original research and data stories are what journalists cover AND what lands content in AI Overviews. For a Hindu/spirituality site, this could mean publishing original surveys, datasets, or documented studies (e.g., festival observance trends, meditation research, scripture translation analyses).
- **Earning 1,000+ earned citations required a systematic digital PR approach**, not passive link building — AI citation gaps can be closed through proactive outreach pairing original data with media-friendly storytelling.
- **AI cyberattack risk is escalating** — over 100 organizations including Google and OpenAI are urging site owners to strengthen security infrastructure. A hacked or flagged site loses AI citation eligibility entirely, making security now a direct GEO concern.

## Key Takeaway for a Spiritual/Religious Content Website

The single most important action this month is to **implement comprehensive, consistent schema markup — especially `Author`, `Article`, and `Organization` structured data — across all existing and new content**, because schema has been explicitly reframed as the primary mechanism by which AI systems like Google's AI Overviews decide whether to trust and cite a source. A Hindu/spirituality website faces an inherent credibility challenge in AI systems that favor established institutional voices, and structured data is now your clearest technical lever to assert entity authority; simultaneously, open Google Search Console's new AI Reports to measure your current AI Overviews impressions so you have a baseline against which every future GEO effort can actually be tracked and validated.
