## SEO Updates

- **Hyphenated domain names confirmed as SEO-neutral by Google**: Google has officially stated that hyphenated domains carry no SEO penalty, debunking a long-standing assumption. If your spiritual website uses or is considering a hyphenated domain (e.g., hindu-dharma-guide.com), this is no longer a concern for rankings.
- **Google has updated its SEO hiring guidance** to actively encourage FTC complaints against misleading SEO providers, and is specifically cautioning against AI-driven SEO services and tools making unverifiable claims. Vet any SEO tools or agencies serving your site carefully against Google's own published guidance, not third-party benchmarks.
- **Google is asserting itself as the authoritative source for SEO, AEO, and GEO best practices**, explicitly questioning the accuracy of third-party SEO tools and data. This means strategies for your site should be grounded in Google Search Central documentation rather than tool-reported metrics alone.

## AEO Updates (Answer Engine Optimization)

- **Google is formally positioning itself as the ground truth for AEO guidance**, signaling that AEO is now mainstream enough to warrant official Google documentation. Spiritual/Q&A-heavy content sites should monitor Google Search Central directly for emerging AEO-specific recommendations rather than relying on third-party interpretations.
- **The CMO/CIO organizational gap around AI agent ecosystems is a real strategic risk**: AEO is now operating at an infrastructure level, not just a content level. For spirituality sites managed by small teams, this is an early signal that AEO decisions (what questions your content answers, how it's structured for agents) need intentional ownership rather than default treatment.

## GEO Updates (Generative Engine Optimization / AI Search)

- **Google is testing an opt-out mechanism for AI Search (AI Overviews/SGE-style features)**, allowing websites to potentially exclude their content from being surfaced in generative responses. However, the critical caveat is that **click and visibility data needed to make an informed opt-out decision is not yet available**, making it premature to opt out without risking blind strategic decisions.
- **AI Search visibility reporting is being tested in Google Search Console**, with early signals that publishers will eventually be able to see how their content performs inside AI-generated answers — a fundamentally new KPI layer beyond traditional click data that spiritual content publishers should prepare to track.
- **The UK is now legally requiring AI search opt-out controls for publishers**, indicating that regulatory pressure on AI content usage is accelerating globally. Spiritual content sites with audiences in regulated markets should monitor compliance requirements around content attribution in AI responses.
- **Tying AI Search to revenue performance is an emerging measurement gap**: Traditional click-based KPIs do not capture AI Search influence. For spirituality sites monetizing through courses, donations, or affiliate links, begin building attribution models that account for zero-click AI-assisted discovery journeys.

## Key Takeaway for a Spiritual/Religious Content Website

The single most important action this month is to **do not opt out of Google's AI Search features yet** — despite the option becoming available. Google's own data shows that the click and visibility metrics needed to evaluate whether AI Search helps or hurts your site simply don't exist yet in any usable form. Instead, use this window to structure your spiritual content — puja guides, scripture explanations, mantra meanings, festival significance — in a clear, question-and-answer format that positions it favorably for AI-generated responses, while closely watching Google Search Central (not third-party tools) for the official AEO and GEO guidance Google has signaled is coming, so you can act from accurate data rather than assumption.
